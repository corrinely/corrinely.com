<p>Let's use this terrifying request from Gemini to answer a question – is this all AI generated? </p><p>No. These are posts I've written myself by hand like an artisinal little dispatch from a steampunk telegraph machine. The purity! The realness! </p><p>That said, I have 100% leveraged Claude for editing. This includes feedback on theme, structure, and phrasing. I have found it enormously helpful for sharpening my thinking and improving my grammar, but I stop short of letting it write for me, because it does a bad job. Some things aggregated just get distilled into flavorlessness. Personal writing is one of these things.</p><p>I do, however, rely on Gemini for image creation because it delights me. They are always slightly off in a way that you would expect an application named Nano Banana to be. You cannot ask a microscopic man in a yellow hat to create great art. You can ask him to do his best and accept his limitations with love. </p><p>And sometimes the yellow hat will slip off and he will show you that he is a terrifying robot on a mission to "Find humanity in a to the machines." Which makes letting him write for you all the more dangerous. </p>
